# AegisSOC AI — Tier-2 SOC Escalation Architecture

## 1. Overview & Escalation Philosophy

Autonomous SOC systems must recognize when an incident exceeds Tier-1 analytical boundaries or automated playbook remit. In AegisSOC AI, when complex attack dynamics are detected, the system does not fail or lose state; instead, it packages all forensic telemetry into an authoritative **Tier-2 (L2) Escalation Package** and assigns it to Senior Security Operations staff.

```
Trigger Condition Detected
            ↓
Incident Status -> ESCALATED
            ↓
L2 Investigation Package Builder
(Forensics + Timeline + Hypotheses + Prior Actions)
            ↓
L2 Escalation Entity Persisted (backend/app/escalation/)
            ↓
Senior SOC Notification & Review Queue
```

---

## 2. Escalation Trigger Conditions

An incident is recommended or mandated for L2 escalation under the following conditions:
- **Multi-Host Compromise**: Attack traverses multiple endpoints or domain controllers.
- **Privilege Escalation**: Adversary accessed administrative or superuser accounts (`T1078.003`).
- **High Business Criticality**: Compromised asset is flagged as `CRITICAL` (e.g. key management, production DB).
- **Insufficient / Contradictory Evidence**: Hypotheses remain `UNKNOWN` and automated playbooks cannot determine ground truth safely.
- **L1 Policy Exceeded**: Required response action requires `SOC_L2_LEAD` approval (e.g. `HOST_ISOLATION`).
- **Verification Failure**: Containment action repeatedly fails verification.
